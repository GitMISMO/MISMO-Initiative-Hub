Work Product Overview

What is the Loan Boarding Data Segment?

The MISMO Loan Boarding Data Segment (LBDS) is a standardized, extensible dataset that defines the core information needed to board newly originated residential mortgage loans into servicing systems in a consistent and reliable way.

The LBDS establishes a common, comprehensive industry baseline at the point where loans transition from origination to servicing — and is the first published piece of MISMO's broader Industry Transfer of Servicing Dataset (ITSD).

Why is MISMO working on this?

  1. Manual loan boarding drives up labor costs. Standardized data means less time spent mapping incoming files by hand — an estimated $12–$60 per loan.
  2. Rework and reconciliation delay activation. Complete, consistent data means fewer post-boarding corrections and faster activation — an estimated $5–$29 per loan in avoided rework and delay.
  3. Inconsistent data leaves borrowers with misdirected payments and account gaps. Consistent data means quicker transfers and a clearer path to the new servicer.
